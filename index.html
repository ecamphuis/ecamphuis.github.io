About Me.

I am a cosmologist working on the cosmic microwave background. For the past five years I have been part of the SPT-3G collaboration, where I led the analysis of the primary CMB anisotropies measured by the South Pole Telescope over its Main field — 1500 square degrees observed in 2019 and 2020. That work produced the SPT-3G D1 temperature and E-mode polarization power spectra, and the cosmological constraints that follow from them: competitive on their own, and tighter still in combination with other datasets.

My work sits where instrument modelling meets statistics. Building an analysis pipeline end to end and validating it against realistic simulations and null tests; computing accurate covariance matrices; developing methods that keep instrumental systematics from leaking into cosmological parameters. Along the way I have released cmbcov, a package for analytical CMB power spectrum covariance matrices, contributed to candl, and built the SPT-3G D1 T&E likelihood. I am joining the University of Melbourne, where I will be based until the end of 2029.

Selected Research Works.

Over the past years, my main focus has been the analysis of the South Pole Telescope main field data acquired between 2019 and 2020, SPT-3G D1, which covers 1500 square degrees of the sky. I have led the intensity and E-mode polarization power spectrum analysis of this dataset and the subsequent cosmological interpretation.

Physical Review D · 2026

SPT-3G D1: CMB temperature and polarization power spectra and cosmology from 2019 and 2020 observations of the SPT-3G Main field

First author — analysis lead

The deepest maps ever used in a CMB TT/TE/EE analysis, covering 4% of the sky, yielding temperature and E-mode polarization power spectra validated through blind and unblind consistency tests. I led this analysis end to end, from the pipeline to the cosmological constraints it delivers on its own and in combination with other datasets.

Astronomy & Astrophysics 668, A62 · 2022

Accurate CMB covariance matrices: exact calculation and approximations

First author

An exact analytical calculation of CMB power spectrum covariance matrices for surveys observing a small footprint, as SPT-3G does with 4% of the sky. The paper also sets out a series of approximations and the regimes in which each of them holds, released as an open-source package.
